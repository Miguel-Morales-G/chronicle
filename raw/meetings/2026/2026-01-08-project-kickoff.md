## Weekly Project Kickoff — Project Aurora

**Date:** 2026-01-08
**Participants:**
- Program Director
- Chief Architect
- Marine Engineering Lead
- Energy Systems Lead

### Discussion Summary

The team aligned on the vision and initial scope for Project Aurora, a permanent floating city prototype.

### Decisions
- Project Aurora will focus on a pilot floating district supporting up to 500 residents.
- The initial deployment location will be in a low-cyclone coastal region.
- A modular platform architecture will be used for future expansion.

### Notes

A detailed infrastructure strategy will be discussed in future meetings.
