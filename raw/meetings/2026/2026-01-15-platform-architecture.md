## Platform Architecture Review — Project Aurora

**Date:** 2026-01-15
**Participants:**
- Chief Architect
- Structural Engineer
- Marine Engineering Lead

### Discussion Summary

The meeting focused on evaluating candidate platform configurations.

### Decisions
- Hexagonal platform modules were selected as the preferred architecture.
- Standard module diameter will be 60 meters.
- Modules will be designed for independent replacement and maintenance.

### Notes

Long-term scalability remains an important design objective.
