# Glossary

## Project Aurora

- **Category:** domain
- **Introduced in:** `raw/meetings/2026/2026-01-08-project-kickoff.md`

Project Aurora is the project initiative focused on developing a permanent floating city prototype. The initial scope centers on a pilot floating district designed to support up to 500 residents.

## Modular Platform Architecture

- **Category:** technical
- **Introduced in:** `raw/meetings/2026/2026-01-08-project-kickoff.md`

Modular platform architecture refers to the planned structural approach for the floating city prototype, where modular platforms are used to enable future expansion of the deployment.

## Microgrid

- **Category:** technical
- **Introduced in:** `raw/meetings/2026/2026-03-19-energy-review.md`

A localized energy network capable of operating independently.

## Centralized Service Center

- **Category:** domain
- **Introduced in:** `raw/meetings/2026/2026-03-26-community-services.md`

A centralized facility designated to support and handle resident service requests within Project Aurora.

## Digital Archive

- **Category:** technical
- **Introduced in:** `raw/meetings/2026/2026-04-09-documentation-standards.md`

Central repository used for long-term preservation of project knowledge.
